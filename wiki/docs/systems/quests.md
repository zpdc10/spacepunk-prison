# Quests and contracts

Quests guide you through every system and give you goals beyond the grind. There are four kinds: the **story**,
**tutorials**, **daily and weekly contracts**, and the monthly **Contract Track**.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/quests` (`/quest`, `/q`, `/questlog`) | Open your quest log |
| `/quests story` / `tutorials` / `contracts` / `track` | Open a tab directly |
| `/contracts` (`/contract`) | Your daily and weekly contracts |

Click a quest in the log to **track** it on your sidebar. The Crew Deck has Quests and Contracts terminals, and the
story characters stand there too: right-click one to open your log.

## The story: "The Warden's Ledger"

Five chapters, one per deck, told by station characters. One step is active at a time, and progress is kept forever,
including through prestige.

| Chapter | Opens at | Told by | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. Slag and Signal | Grade 1 | Foreman Rusk, Scrapyard Foreman | Fresh Intake, Scrip, Clearance, Something Under the Rust, Evidence, Teeth, Clearance to the Ducts |
| II. Hot Pipes | Grade 6 | Tech Vell, Reactor Technician | Rusk's Courier, Ghost Payroll, Redline, Heat Sink, Data Spike, Sealed Deck |
| III. Cold Storage | Grade 11 | Archivist Nhem, Cold Records | Thaw, The Index, Lapis Record, Frozen Payroll, Ice Cores, Breach Clearance |
| IV. Vacuum Debt | Grade 16 | Kade, Breach Smuggler | Entry Fee, Quartz Run, Breach Shift, Tooling Up, The Debris Field, Black Box, Into the Foundry |
| V. The Warden's Ledger | Grade 21 | THE WARDEN, Station Intelligence | It Heard You, Debt Collection, Neutronium, Melt the Books, Core Access, Clean Slate |

Steps ask you to mine, sell, rank up, find items, hand in cargo and more. Rewards are Credits (often a share of your
next rank-up cost), Tokens and, late in the story, Shards. The last step, **Clean Slate**, asks for your first
prestige and pays **500 Tokens and 10 Shards**.

## Daily and weekly contracts

- **3 dailies** reset at midnight; **3 weeklies** reset Monday at midnight (server time).
- They are rolled from pools that scale with your grade, and only from systems you can use.
- Finishing **all three dailies** pays a bonus: 10 contract points plus extra Tokens.
- Contract Credits are a share of your next rank-up cost, so they stay useful at every grade.

**Daily pool:** Pit Shift (mine in your best mine), Deck Sweep (mine anywhere on your deck), Ore Quota (mine a
specific ore), Drill Tuning (buy 2 enchant levels), Overtime (play 45 minutes), Maintenance Run (finish a run on your
deck), Catch of the Day (catch 20 fish), Line Work (process items in your workshop), Salvage Hunt (find a rare drop)
and Undercroft Brawl (win 2 Undercroft fights). Each pays about 10 to 12 points.

**Weekly pool:** Deck Haul (20,000+ blocks on your deck), Quarterly Numbers (sell 2.5× your next rank-up cost),
Promotion (rank up twice), Reliable Contractor (finish 10 dailies), Bulk Delivery (hand in 1,500+ of an ore from your
cargo hold), Gantry Runner (finish 4 runs), Overhaul (buy 10 enchant levels), Double Shift (play 300 minutes),
Trawler (catch 200 fish), Production Run (process 1,000+ items) and Pit Fighter (win 15 Undercroft fights). Each pays
about 40 to 45 points.

**Deliver** objectives take ore straight from your cargo hold when you click the contract in the log.

Every finished daily or weekly contract has a 60% chance of a [scrap part](rare-drops.md#scrap-parts), and every
story step gives one. The Encrypted Data Core part only comes from contracts and the story.

## The Contract Track

Contract points add up on a **monthly track of 30 tiers** (40 to 1,200 points), which resets on the 1st. Claim tiers in
`/quests track`. A daily player earns about 40 points a day plus about 120 a week.

| Points | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Every 40 | Tokens, and on alternate tiers Credits (a growing share of your next rank-up) |
| 400 | Big Token tier + **2 Shards** |
| 600 | Big Token tier + **Cryo Coolant Loop** module |
| 800 | Big Token tier + **3 Shards** |
| 1,200 | Big Token tier + **5 Shards** + **Magnet Coil** module |

## How it ties into progression

Quest and contract Credits are server income, so they count like selling. Contracts push you to try every system,
the track is a steady source of Shards and modules, and finished quests count toward your
[gang's](gangs.md) weekly contracts.
