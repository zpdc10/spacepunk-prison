# Rules of the station

How the world itself behaves, everywhere on the server.

## Vanilla client, required pack

You play with a normal Java Edition client and no mods. The **station texture pack is required**: decline it and
you're kicked with instructions to enable it. It's built into the server, so it updates automatically.

## The map is protected

Every world is protected by default, for everyone, ops included. You can only break and place blocks where a system
allows it:

- inside **mine pits**,
- inside **your own workshop bay** (and bays you're crew on),
- on Undercroft **hot ore veins**.

Everything else (the station, mine rooms, parkour courses, fishing pools) can't be changed. Staff use `/buildmode`
to edit the map.

## PvP only in the Undercroft

PvP is off everywhere except the [Undercroft](systems/undercroft.md) rings. Guns don't fire anywhere else. Gangmates
can never hurt each other.

## Soulbound gear

Your **drill** and your **fishing rod** are soulbound: they can't be dropped, stored, put in item frames, traded or
lost on death, and you keep them through prestige.

## No weather

It never rains or storms in any world. The station holds a dark, starry midnight sky.

## Respawning

You respawn on the station concourse. Dying in the Undercroft drops everything but your drill.
