# Getting started

## Joining

1. Add the server and join with a normal Java Edition client. No mods are needed.
2. Accept the **station texture pack** when asked. It is required: declining it kicks you with instructions. If the
   prompt doesn't appear, select the server in your server list, click **Edit**, and set **Server Resource Packs**
   to **Enabled**. `/resourcepack` (`/rp`) re-sends it.
3. You arrive on the **station concourse**, the hub of everything. You respawn here too.

## Your first hour

1. **Get your drill.** Every player gets one soulbound drill on first join. It can't be dropped, stored or lost,
   and you keep it forever, through prestige too.
2. **Go mining.** Take the **Mine Lifts** (north wing) or type `/mine`. It sends you to the best mine your grade
   unlocks. Grade 1 is the **Slag Heap** on the Scrapyard deck.
3. **Fill your cargo hold.** Mined ore goes straight into your [cargo hold](systems/selling.md), not your
   inventory. A boss bar shows how full it is.
4. **Sell.** Type `/sell` or use a sell terminal in the Exchange (east wing). You get
   <span class="cur credits">Credits</span>.
5. **Rank up.** `/rankup` buys your next clearance grade. Grade 2 costs 4,000 Credits and opens the Rust Pit.
6. **Upgrade your drill.** Shift + right-click the drill (or `/drill`) to open the drill bay. Spend
   <span class="cur tokens">Tokens</span> on enchants like Bore Speed and Yield.
7. **Follow the quests.** `/quests` opens your quest log. The story, "The Warden's Ledger", starts right away, and
   tutorials start on their own as you unlock each system.

## Things to try early

| What | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily maintenance run | A free reward roll in every mine, once a day | [Parkour](systems/parkour.md), `/parkour` |
| Daily contracts | Credits, Tokens and points toward the monthly Contract Track | [Quests](systems/quests.md), `/contracts` |
| Fishing | A second income, and the only source of Scales | [Fishing](systems/fishing.md), Shuttle Bay or `/waters` |
| Your workshop | Process ore into goods worth more than raw ore | [Workshop](systems/workshop.md), `/ws` |
| Salvage | Rare finds while you mine: caches, relics, blueprints | [Rare drops](systems/rare-drops.md), `/salvage` |

## How long it takes

The first run from grade 1 to grade 25 is tuned for about **70 hours of active play** (the target is 50 to 100).
The first rank-up takes about half an hour, the last about 9 hours. Each grade takes roughly 12% longer than the one
before. Workshop income, fishing and contracts make it faster.
