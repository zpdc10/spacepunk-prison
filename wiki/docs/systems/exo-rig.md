# Exo-Rig

The **Exo-Rig** is a module rig for your body. It's **always on**, whatever you're doing: mining, fishing, running
parkour or fighting in the Undercroft. It uses the same grid system as the drill's
[Module Rig](drill.md#3-modules-and-the-module-rig): shaped modules, rotation, families, placement rules, hidden combos
and Mk I to Mk V fusing.

Open it with `/rig exo`, or the **Exo-Rig** tab in the rig menu.

## Frames

Buy frames in order with the button on the right of the rig. A frame is kept forever, prestige included.

| Frame | Unlocks at | Cost | Grid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scrap Harness | Grade 5 | 60K Credits | 2×2 |
| Field Exo | Grade 14 | 6M Credits | 3×2 |
| Combat Exo | Prestige 1 | 10 Shards | 3×3, 1 core cell |
| Prototype Exo | Prestige 5 | 50 Shards | 4×3, 1 core cell |

## What it changes

Exo modules change your character's stats:

- **Speed**, **max health** (every +10% is +2 HP, one heart), **armor**, **knockback resistance**, **jump height**
  and **fall damage** (lower is better).
- **Ore sell price**.
- **Gadget recharge time** (lower is better).

## Gadgets

Gadget modules give you an active ability. **Sneak + F** (swap hands) fires it, as long as you aren't holding a gun.
A rig can only run **one** gadget.

| Gadget | What it does | Recharge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grapple Launcher | Dash forward | 8s |
| Scanner Pulse | Players and mobs within 24 blocks glow for 6 seconds | 30s |
| Stim Injector | Regeneration II for 6 seconds, plus absorption hearts | 45s |
| Phase Cloak | 5 seconds of invisibility and speed | 60s |

## Where exo modules come from

- **Daily parkour:** 12% on every reward roll.
- **Contracts:** 6% for every contract you finish.
- **Assembly Bench:** build any exo module whose schematic you know at your
  [workshop](workshop.md#the-assembly-bench). Everyone starts knowing Servo Greaves.

Like every module, they fuse up to Mk V, can be traded, and are kept through prestige.
