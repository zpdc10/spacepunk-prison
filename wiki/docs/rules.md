# Rules of the station

How the world itself behaves, everywhere on the server.

## Vanilla client, required pack

You play with a normal Java Edition client and no mods. The **station texture pack is required**: decline it and
you're kicked with instructions to enable it. It's built into the server, so it updates automatically.

## The map is protected

Every world is protected by default, for everyone, ops included. You can only break and place blocks where a system
allows it:

- inside **mine pits**,
- inside **your own workshop bay** (and bays you're crew on),
- on Undercroft **hot ore veins**.

Everything else (the station, mine rooms, parkour courses, fishing pools) can't be changed. Staff use `/buildmode`
to edit the map.

## PvP only in the Undercroft

PvP is off everywhere except the [Undercroft](systems/undercroft.md) rings. Guns don't fire anywhere else. Gangmates
can never hurt each other.

## Soulbound gear

Your **drill** and your **fishing rod** are soulbound: they can't be dropped, stored, put in item frames, traded or
lost on death, and you keep them through prestige.

## You can't die outside the Undercroft

Anywhere outside the Undercroft's PvP rings (the station, mines, parkour, fishing decks, workshop bays and the
Undercroft's Guard Checkpoint) you take no fall, void, lava, fire, drowning, suffocation or hunger damage, and your
food bar never drains.

- Fall into the void and a drone puts you back on the station concourse.
- Get caught inside blocks (say, a mine refilling around you) and you're lifted to the nearest open air.
- Sea creatures on the fishing decks can still hit you, but never for a killing blow.
- Parkour still works the same: fall off and you're sent back to your last checkpoint.

If you somehow do die outside a PvP zone, you keep your whole inventory and your XP.

## No weather, no stray mobs

It never rains or storms in any world, and time never moves on (the station holds a dark, starry midnight sky).
No vanilla mobs spawn anywhere: no bats in the mines, no phantoms, raids, patrols or wandering traders. The only
creatures you'll meet are the station's own (quest NPCs and fishing sea creatures). Fire doesn't spread and
advancements aren't announced in chat.

## Respawning

You respawn on the station concourse. Dying in the Undercroft drops everything but your drill.
