# Prestige

Prestige is the long-term goal of every run. At **grade 25** you can reset to grade 1 in exchange for
<span class="cur shards">Prestige Shards</span>, a permanent sell bonus and new unlocks.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/prestige` | Shows what you'd get, then asks you to confirm |
| `/prestige confirm` | Prestige (within 30 seconds of `/prestige`) |

## What resets and what you keep

| Resets | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your grade (back to 1) | Your drill: frame, enchants and modules |
| Your Credits (wiped) | Tokens, Shards and Scales |
| What's in your cargo hold | Cargo hold upgrades and the Auto-Seller |
| Credits you parked in a gang bank this run | Your workshop bay, machines and locker |
| | Fishing level, rod, collections; relic collections; quest progress |

!!! warning "Spend your Credits first"
    Prestige wipes your wallet. Buy what you need (workshop machines, cargo upgrades, bait, guns) before you
    confirm. Parking Credits in a gang bank doesn't save them either: see [Gangs](gangs.md).

## What each prestige gives

Prestiging to level P pays:

- **<span class="cur shards">10 + 2 × P Shards</span>**: 12 at P1, 14 at P2, 20 at P5, 30 at P10, 40 at P15, 50 at P20.
- **+10% sell price, permanently**, per prestige level.
- **+15% on every Token find, permanently**, per prestige level, compounding: ×1.15 at P1, ×2 at P5, ×4 at P10.
- **Unlocks** at certain levels:

| Prestige | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Gangs (found or join a crew); the Industrial drill frame; Smuggler's Run (Undercroft ring 2); the Plasma Lance |
| 3 | The Sump (Undercroft ring 3) |
| 5 | The Military drill frame |
| 15 | The Prototype drill frame |
| Every level | One more Black Market stall (3 + 1 per prestige, up to 14) |

- **Status:** your prestige shows in front of your name in the tab list, e.g. `[P3 G12]`.

!!! info "Prestige doesn't pay Tokens directly"
    Tokens come from activities, mainly mining (see [Currencies](../currencies.md#tokens)). Prestige makes every
    find bigger instead: mining earns about 62K Tokens over a Prestige 1 run, 185K at P5 and 480K at P10.

## The next run is harder

Every rank-up costs **50% more per prestige level**: ×1.5 on your second run, ×2 on your third. Your bigger sell bonus,
better drill and workshop make up much of the difference. Prestige goes up to level 100.

## Story

The last step of the story questline, "Clean Slate", asks for your first prestige and pays 500 Tokens and 10 Shards.
