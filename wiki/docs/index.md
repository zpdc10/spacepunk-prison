# Spacepunk Prison

You are an inmate on a derelict orbital penal station. Your sentence pays out in ore. Mine your way down five rusted
decks toward the reactor core, rank up your clearance, rebuild your drill, and prestige to start again stronger.

Everything runs on a plain Java Edition client. The server sends a **required resource pack** when you join, which
gives the station its look: drills, ores, guns, machines, fish and menus.

!!! tip "New here?"
    Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md), then read [Currencies](currencies.md). Every other page
    explains one system: what it is, the commands, how you earn things, what you spend them on, and how it feeds
    your progression.

## The core loop

1. **Mine** ore in your grade's mine with your drill.
2. **Sell** the haul for <span class="cur credits">Credits</span>, and pick up <span class="cur tokens">Tokens</span> now and then.
3. **Rank up** with Credits to unlock deeper, richer mines, and **upgrade the drill** with Tokens.
4. **Prestige** at grade 25: your grade and Credits reset, you earn <span class="cur shards">Prestige Shards</span> and a permanent sell bonus.

Everything else on the station plugs into that loop: more ways to earn Credits, more Tokens, more Shards, and
better tools.
